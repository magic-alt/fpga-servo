## Current definitions and fuse budget

At the peak setting, phase RMS is20/√2=14.142 A. For linear SVPWM and unity power factor, maximum fundamental phase voltage RMS is Vbus/√6. Thus ideal **average** input current≤3(Vbus/√6)IphaseRMS/Vbus=√1.5 IphaseRMS:12.247 A continuous or17.321 A during the30 s setting, before losses. This is not a fuse-RMS proof. Input ripple depends on the ten-can bank, cable/source impedances and switching spectrum. For balanced phase currents, each inverter active switching state selects one phase current or its negative; a conservative unfiltered switching-current bound is|Ibus|≤√2 IphaseRMS, hence RMS≤14.142/20 A. No passive-network resonance amplification is included in that bound.

An illustrative85% auxiliary efficiency at full12 W load adds0.588 A at24 V or0.294 A at48 V. Minkowski's conservative RMS sum gives F1/Q7/Q8 design-current estimates14.730 A continuously and20.588 A in the30 s regime at24 V. Actual efficiency, converter startup and regeneration currents must be measured;85% is an assumption, not an OEM guarantee.

## MOSFETs, shunts and switching loss

The selected [BSC040N10NS5 Rev2.4, pp3–5 and8](https://www.infineon.com/dgdl/Infineon-BSC040N10NS5-DataSheet-v02_04-EN.pdf?fileId=5546d4624a0bf290014a0fbcee2e6b38) specifies4 milliohm maximum at10 V gate drive/25°C, a temperature-dependent resistance curve,150°C junction maximum and a conditional50 K/W example board. Its large case-controlled current rating is not this PCB's current rating. Each phase shunt dissipates30 J during the30 s peak regime. The2 W value at20 A is the instantaneous crest loss, not the30 s average.60 J each would correspond to an extra20 Arms/DC envelope. At the nominal+25 A OCP threshold, RSH4 dissipates1.25 W while that current persists. The selected HoYLR2512 parts are3 W catalog selections; exact terminal temperature/land/power derating and real soldered thermal resistance remain unqualified. Two-terminal shunts need true separate inner-edge Kelvin pickups. Board copper temperature and sense error cannot be inferred from the catalog wattage.

Switching loss is potentially larger than conduction. A sensitivity calculation using external33 ohm, assumed driver5 ohm, internal gate2 ohm,12 V drive,4.6 V plateau and18 nC Miller charge gives approximately254 ns total Miller transition time. With P≈6×0.5Vbus×mean|Iphase|×(ton+toff)×20kHz, at48 V this is6.58 W continuously or9.31 W at the peak setting. These are not measured loss values or guaranteed upper bounds: the assumed driver impedance, nonlinear gate charge, current/voltage conditions and parasitic turn-on can change them. A1.1 V diode-drop sensitivity with200 ns–1 us deadtime adds approximately0.24–1.19 W /0.34–1.68 W. Coss, reverse recovery, avalanche, driver and copper losses are additional; actual deadtime control does not exist in this repository yet.

Consequently a first thermal allocation already totals roughly16–18 W continuously or28–30 W at the30 s setting when the above hot-conduction, overlap and deadtime sensitivities, shunts and damping losses are summed, before remaining losses and auxiliary conversion heat. This is a cooling-design estimate, not a rating. The final board's copper stack, airflow, local hot spots and mutual heating must be specified/measured. Junction and shunt temperatures cannot be established by multiplying every part's example-board thermal resistance independently. Gate VGS, switching time/loss and temperature must be observed at low energy before raising current.

## Controlled startup, SOA and regeneration

The [LM74502 RevA, pp4–6 and11–12](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm74502.pdf) requires input POR, enable and charge-pump UV release before gate drive. The POR rising3.9 V is typical, and pump release is5.7–7.5 V above VS. The60-microampere gate drive is not a precise current limiter. A nominal5 V turn-on assumption is not guaranteed. Its VS absolute limit is65 V and it has no reverse-current blocking. Thus72 V capacitor energy calculations do not establish a permitted assembled-bus voltage. A protected-bus external sink via J7 must hold the actual bus≤60 V including overshoot/tolerances, preserving margin to65 V, under regeneration and input loss. A J3-only sink cannot be assumed to protect the protected side when the input switch is off.

**Revision2: two-stage prototype fixture.** This supersedes the unqualified direct48 V startup case. Previous versions and source hashes are preserved in `fixture_evidence_2026-10-19/`. The low-voltage step is fixture precharge, not a10 V operating rating for this24–48 V drive.

**Qualify the fixture before connecting the board.** At the eventual DUT terminals, verify stage1 voltage≤10 V and total source current≤0.1 A, including current-limit overshoot and discharge of PSU/output/cable capacitance. Characterize a short-circuit dummy load and1.2 mF dummy capacitor, recording transient waveforms and probe calibration. Front-panel settings alone do not establish these bounds. A large passive series resistor is not assumed: its voltage drop could prevent the onboard auxiliary load from reaching9.5 V. Fixture qualification is **NOT_RUN**; if the terminal bounds cannot be established, STOP before connecting the DUT.

**Stage1 — low-voltage precharge.** Disconnect motor, encoder and all external12 V loads. Inhibit the bridge independently of future software. Connect the fixture at0 V, then source≤10 V with≤0.1 A total compliance. Do not raise either limit to force startup. Independently measure TP1/VBUS_PROT and both Q7/Q8 VGS/VDS. Advance only after TP1≥9.5 V is stable, each VGS≥10 V and each |VDS|≤0.10 V under the available stage1 load. The VGS criterion corresponds to the4-milliohm specification; low VDS alone at near-zero current does not prove enhancement. Record current, bus voltage, both gate/source/drain measurements and temperature. Failure to reach any condition means STOP, remove power and investigate. Onboard load may legitimately prevent successful startup at0.1 A. No turn-on by5 V is assumed.

These verified terminal bounds give total input power≤1 W and any individual Q7/Q8 dissipation≤1 W at VDS≤10 V and current≤0.1 A, without assuming equal energy division. Cmax=1.2 mF stores≤0.060 J at10 V. Ideal late charging at10 V/0.1 A takes0.120 s and dissipates≤0.060 J in the total series path. A persistent short/stalled startup can continue dissipating up to1 W; its energy is not limited to the capacitor charge energy. Monitor both cases and stop for case temperature≥60°C or unexpected temperature rise. That is a conservative fixture stop, not an inferred junction-temperature measurement.
