# Same-value gate resistor ECO

Both boards now select **Vishay CRCW060333R0FKEAHP**, 33 Ω, 1%, instead of the general-purpose RS-03K33R0FT. The resistance, logical circuitry and every native physical pin/net membership are unchanged. Only RG1–RG6 identity/sourcing and footprint assignment change. Exact proof is in [the native comparison](gate_resistor_eco_verification.json). Unreviewed alternates are disabled.

The [manufacturer datasheet](https://www.vishay.com/docs/20043/crcwhpe3.pdf), document20043 revision17-Mar-2026, was downloaded and visually checked: page1 ratings, page3 ordering code, page5 **continuous** pulse curve, and page9 dimensions/reflow lands. SHA-256 is `0f80613bc765f1266801a1afe1505d2ece7cb4125fd730294bc6a5167d02c25a`. The selected 0603 part is0.33 W at70°C; linear derating to155°C gives0.252 W at90°C. The continuous-pulse curve and average-power/voltage conditions apply together; the single-pulse curve does not justify repetitive switching.

The local footprint follows the current OEM reflow recommendation: each pad0.75×1.00 mm, gap0.75 mm, pad centres±0.75 mm. Body is1.6±0.1×0.85±0.1 mm, height0.45±0.1 mm. The previous generic footprint centres±0.825 mm remain within the new pads, but this is only an integration expectation: native replacement, refill, DRC and all six gate connections must be checked on the final boards.

A **conditional normal-operation measurement envelope**, not an OEM MOSFET charge guarantee, is supply≤12.514 V, at most two gate-resistor pulses per50 µs PWM cycle, each bounded by a1 µs rectangle at approximately4.85 W. This gives approximately0.194 W average, below the90°C allocation. The OEM continuous-pulse curve exceeds this rectangle comfortably. These conservative rectangles are a qualification target: actual VGS, resistor voltage/current, pulse duration, rail overshoot and local temperature remain **NOT_RUN**. MOSFET Qg specified at10 V cannot prove the12.5 V waveform; ringing and a failed/shorted gate are outside this argument. The ECO closes a missing pulse-rating source, not gate-drive dynamics or fault qualification.
